Why we honor Charan

For shaping consequential technology and intellectual-property transactions while bringing the same commitment to advocacy, public service, and the communities the profession can serve.

Meet Charan

Charan Sandhu is Head of Weil’s Technology & IP Transactions practice and a member of the Firm’s Privacy & Cybersecurity group and is based in New York. Charan concentrates on the areas of complex technology transactions and intellectual property. She represents clients in matters relating to technology transfer and the development, acquisition, use and commercial exploitation of technology and intellectual property. She has extensive experience in a wide variety of complex technology transactions, including technology development and licensing agreements, joint ventures, strategic alliances, professional services agreements, outsourcing arrangements, distribution agreements and settlements of IP litigation.

Charan is ranked Band 1 for Technology in New York and Band 2 for Technology nationwide by Chambers USA, where clients note that “having her on the team is like having the best of both worlds, with the resources of outside counsel and the insights and understanding of in-house counsel,” and praise her for her “practical and extremely knowledgeable” approach to IP and technology transactions, as well as her “ability to get deals done and to understand the issues and their place in the wider corporate context.” She was named the 2025 “North America Technology Lawyer of the Year” by Euromoney: Women in Business Law Americas.

Charan is recommended for Healthcare: Life Sciences and named to the Hall of Fame for Media, Technology and Telecoms: Technology Transactions by Legal 500 US, which also recognizes her as a “Leading Lawyer” for Technology Transactions and for Patents: Licensing. Clients commend her as “a great strategist” with “a deep understanding of her client’s business needs.” She is further recognized by IAM Licensing 250 as one of the “World’s Leading Patent & Technology Licensing Lawyers,” and is named a “Global Leader” in New York by the IAM Patent 1000 Global Leaders Guide, where she is also rated “Highly Recommended” and described as “an amazing lawyer and an outstanding drafter” who excels at building relationships with counterparties.

In 2025, Charan received the “Pioneer Award” from the South Asian Bar Association of North America, recognizing her professional excellence and innovation in the practice of law. Managing Intellectual Property’s IP Stars have named her a “Trademark Star” and “Transactions Star,” and included her among the 2023 “Top 250 Women in IP.” She was also named the 2023 “Practitioner of the Year” for IP Transactions in the United States.

Charan is recognized in Lawdragon’s “500 Leading Dealmakers in America”, “500 Leading Lawyers in America,” “500 Leading Global Cyber Lawyers”, and “500 Leading Global IP Lawyers” lists. In 2024, she was a winner of the Corporate Counsel’s Women, Influence & Power in Law Awards in the “Mentor & Mentee Collaboration” category, honoring leaders who advance the empowerment of women in law.

Her additional honors include the 2023 “Trailblazer Award” from the South Asian Bar Association of New Jersey and the “Corporate Leadership Award” from the South Asian Bar Association of New York. She was ranked fifth for handling 15 deals in the 2021 “Top Intellectual Property Lawyers in North America” by MergerLinks, named a “Life Sciences Star” by LMG Life Sciences, recognized as a 2019 “Notable Women in Law” by Crain’s New York Business, and honored as Minority Female Lawyer of the Year at the Chambers USA 2018 Women in Law Awards. She has also been named among the 2015 “Top Women” for Intellectual Property in New York by Super Lawyers and was shortlisted for “Best in Technology” at the Euromoney Legal Media Group’s Americas Women in Business Law Awards.

Charan is a member of the Firm’s Pro Bono Committee, Corporate Department Pro Bono Steering Committee, and AsianAttorneys@Weil, Weil’s global affinity group open to all attorneys who are committed to the mission of attracting, retaining, and developing Asian talent, and is the recipient of the Firm’s Ira M. Millstein Award for Excellence in Public Service. She regularly handles pro bono work for Sikh Coalition, Start Small Think Big and Kids in Need of Defense (KIND). Charan was inducted into the YWCA’s Academy of Women Leaders for her outstanding professional achievement, civic commitment and leadership. She was also featured in Profiles in Diversity Journal’s 11th Annual WomenWorthWatching® issue. Charan serves on the Board of Governors of the South Asian Bar Association.

“To be the best at everything I do and empower others to do the same.”

Charan Sandhu
