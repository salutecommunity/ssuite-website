Why we honor Anu

For leadership at the center of global dealmaking, a career built on judgment and relationships, and a commitment to institutions that create opportunity beyond the transaction.

Meet Anu

Anu Aiyengar is the Global Chair of Investment Banking at J.P. Morgan. Since joining the firm in 1999, she has advised on more than $1 trillion in transactions and held a number of senior leadership positions across the Investment Bank.

Prior to her current role, Anu served as Global Head of Advisory and Mergers & Acquisitions, where she was a member of the firm's Global Banking Operating Committee and Global Investment Banking Management team. She led a market-leading global advisory platform of more than 500 bankers across 45 countries, spanning M&A, Corporate Advisory, and Director Advisory Services. Her leadership reflects nearly three decades of transaction experience, including 12 years leading the M&A practice across North America and globally.

Anu has been widely recognized for her leadership and impact. She has been named to Barron's 100 Most Influential Women in U.S. Finance and American Banker's Most Powerful Women in Finance for five consecutive years. Her honors also include the Asian American Business Award, Ascend A-List Honoree, Dress for Success Impact and Innovation Award, and recognition on the Forbes India W-Power List. In 2025, she became the first woman to rank No. 1 on Business Insider's Rainmakers list of top U.S. M&A bankers.

Beyond her professional achievements, Anu is deeply committed to education and community service. She serves as Vice Chair of the Board of Trustees of Smith College and sits on the boards of the New York City Ballet, American Red Cross, and Dress for Success. She holds a B.A. in Economics from Smith College and an MBA from Vanderbilt University.

“When you see a person become the best version of themselves, that’s a high, and that’s fun.”

Anu Aiyengar
