Why we honor Anjula

For connecting culture, technology, and capital—and creating new pathways for South Asian talent and women entrepreneurs to reach audiences, investment, and opportunity.

Meet Anjula

Anjula Acharia is a trailblazing entrepreneur, talent strategist, investor, and cultural catalyst known for building global platforms and cross-cultural careers. Her unique work sits at the intersection of celebrity, technology, and culture, shaping who and what we pay attention to on a global stage.

As the founder and CEO of A-Series Investments and Management, Acharia has shattered barriers, forging a path for women entrepreneurs and championing a wide range of women-led and women-owned businesses. She is also a strategic investor and advisor to dozens of innovative consumer tech and CPG startups, with two of her early-stage investments—ClassPass and Bumble—growing into billion-dollar unicorns. Under A-Series, Acharia has also been the longtime manager of actress Priyanka Chopra Jonas and played a pivotal role in launching her U.S. career, from Bollywood actress to being the first Indian lead in a U.S. network drama and achieving true global stardom. Her track record for connecting talent with global opportunity is unmatched.

She also founded Desi Hits!, a platform that connected music labels and artists such as Lady Gaga and 50 Cent to the Indian market. The Indian Government recognized Acharia for her leadership in cross-cultural partnerships between India & the U.S. Further expanding her mission to elevate South Asian voices, Acharia recently partnered with Warner Music Group to launch 5 Junction, a new record label she founded to discover and nurture U.S.-based artists of South Asian heritage, while amplifying WMG’s existing roster of South Asian artists.

“My motivation comes from following a mission that brings more unity through diversity.”

Anjula Acharia
