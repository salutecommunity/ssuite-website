Recognizing a South Asian man and a non–South Asian leader for sustained allyship that advances South Asian women into meaningful roles and opportunities.

Why we honor Prisca

For turning partnerships into a platform for belonging—and bringing institutions, philanthropy, and community together around greater safety, visibility, and opportunity.

Meet Prisca

Prisca Bae is a noted diversity, equity and social-impact leader who excels at forging collaborations between corporations, non-profits, and philanthropic initiatives. She is founding team member of The Asian American Foundation and currently serves as the organizations Chief Partnerships Officer. Prior to TAAF, Prisca held leadership roles at PepsiCo, Seneca Point Global, Newsweek and Goldman Sachs. Prisca began her career at Latham & Watkins and served as Federal Law Clerk to Senior District Court Judge William Hoevler in Florida's Southern District.​ During her time at PepsiCo, she spearheaded the development and launch of a landmark $100 million investment in women and girls globally, while also advancing gender parity and pay equity initiatives across the organization. While at Goldman Sachs, she launched Goldman Sachs Gives, a billion-dollar donor advised fund available to current and former partners of the firm.​ She currently serves on the boards of The Roger Lehecka Double Discovery Center at Columbia University, the Korean American Community Foundation and "The Li.st"​ Prisca is a graduate of Columbia University, and the UC Berkley School of Law. She was born in South Korea, was raised in Chicago, and is currently based in New York City.

“If it's the thing that you want to do right now, you should do it, and if it means you get to help just one person, then it will have been worth it.”

Prisca Bae
