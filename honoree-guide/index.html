HONOREE GUIDE

Honoree Run of Show

How will I know when to go to the stage?

Indra Nooyi’s Chair recognition takes place in the opening program, presented by Anu Aiyengar immediately before their fireside conversation. SALUTE delivers the welcome remarks; Anu presents Indra’s recognition and moderates the fireside.

You do not need to watch the clock or make your own way to the stage. For the post-dinner honors, Shaon and a SALUTE Fellow will come to you and your presenter and escort you side stage one award ahead of your presentation.

Once there, you will wait offstage while the short introduction video SALUTE created for you plays. After the video, Shaon and the SALUTE Fellow will cue you and your presenter to enter together. Please wait for their cue rather than walking onstage on your own. The presentation sequence below explains what happens next.

Who is presenting my award?

We have emailed each honoree separately to introduce them to their presenter and outline their presentation details. Each presenter has also received their assigned congratulatory line; no additional presenter remarks are needed. If you have any questions or changes, kindly let us know at shaon@salute.community.

What happens during my award presentation?

The standard post-dinner live presentation follows these steps:

1. Introduction video. The short introduction video SALUTE created for you will play first. You and your presenter will wait side stage during the video.

2. Stage entrance. Once the video ends, you and your presenter come onstage together. Shaon and the SALUTE Fellow will guide your entrance.

3. Congratulations and award handoff. The presenter delivers their assigned congratulatory line into the microphone and hands over the award. There is no additional presenter speech.

4. Photograph. Pause together with the award for the onstage photograph before the presenter exits.

5. Acceptance. Once the presenter has exited, the honoree gives their one-minute acceptance remarks. An award stand and lectern are available; you do not need to hold the award while speaking.

6. Exit. At the end of your remarks, leave the stage so the next recognition can begin. The team will confirm the exit route at the venue.

How long should my acceptance remarks be?

Honoree remarks are limited to one minute so that every honoree has an opportunity to speak. Please prepare your remarks with this limit in mind.

Can I use notes or set down my award?

An award stand and a lectern are available. SALUTE can print remarks cards; please coordinate with Shaon if you would like this arranged.

OUR COMMUNITY

About SALUTE & S.SUITE

What is SALUTE?

South Asian Leaders Unite to Empower (SALUTE) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and private professional network focused on expanding access, visibility, and connection for South Asian women leaders and allies. Founded in 2021, our members are executives, trailblazers, and pioneers who are shaping the present and influencing the future.

SALUTE serves as the connective tissue across the ecosystem—building leadership infrastructure that brings accomplished leaders together and connects them to the broader network.

SALUTE is built to move outward, not inward. We are building a leadership infrastructure that brings accomplished leaders together and connects them to the broader ecosystem—because changing who leads cannot happen within one network. It requires organizations working together to open doors, expand access, and move opportunity beyond the boundaries of any one community.

What does SALUTE’s community look like?

SALUTE has brought together 200+ members over the years, representing 14 industries and 15 professional functions, with an average of 15 years of experience. Access, curation, and connection are the principles SALUTE is built on. We create a community where leaders open doors for one another across industries, functions, and generations.

Why did SALUTE create S.SUITE Honors?

S.SUITE is SALUTE’s inaugural honors ceremony recognizing South Asian leaders and allies across business, capital, and culture.

S.SUITE celebrates their leadership and achievements while expanding visibility for the next generation.

South Asians have always been in the room. What's missing is the spotlight. Recognition is how the narrative shifts — when we name greatness out loud, we make it possible for the next generation to imagine themselves there too. S.SUITE exists to make that visible.

The S.SUITE Class is designed as a leadership cohort that will champion a shared community initiative over the following year.
