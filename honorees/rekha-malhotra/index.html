DJ Rekha

DJ, Producer, Curator, and Educator

S.SUITE Culture

S.SUITE Honors by SALUTE

A leader in arts, media, and public platforms whose work shapes how South Asians are seen and whose stories are centered.

Why we honor DJ Rekha

For shaping how South Asian culture is heard and seen—and building community, on the dance floor and beyond, for generations of artists and audiences.

Biography

DJ Rekha (born Rekha Malhotra) is a Queens, NYC- based DJ, producer, and curator. Their dynamic DJ sets incorporate South Asian Diasporic dance music with ears to a global dance floor. They have DJed across the globe sharing the stage with AR Rahman, Yoko Ono, the Roots, M.I.A and more, and have performed in festivals, clubs, and community spaces worldwide.

Their artistic work is centered in community and they have been instrumental to the early formation of SAYA (South Asian Youth Action), SAWCC (South Asian Women’s Creative Collective). They served as board member for Chhaya CDC. They were part of Sakhi for South Asian Women’s inaugural intern cohort. They have raised money for numerous causes including Malcom X Grassroots Movements, Pakistan Earthquake Relief, and they Minnesota Bail Fund.

In 1997, they founded one of NYC’s longest-running club nights, Basement Bhangra™. Their debut album "DJ Rekha presents Basement Bhangra" featured Wyclef, among other artists. Their score and original music composition includes remixes for Meredith Monk, and Jesse Cook, as well as creating the opening theme (with Raj Makhija and David Sharma) for Padma Lakshmi’s Hulu show, “Taste the Nation, ” and music for the film Americanish. Along with Krithi they produced the closing credit track for The A-List (HBO), in which DJ Rekha is also an onscreen participant.

Additional film and TV credits range from lending their parents’ house for the location of Chutney Popcorn (1999), playing themself in American Desi (2001) and serving as an EP on The Gas Station Attendant (2025), which premiered at Sheffield DocFest to a Grand Jury Special Mention Award and won Best Documentary Feature at Nashville and San Diego Asian Film Festival and as Executive Producer for Saleem Gondal’s debut feature, A Long Jazeera (in production).

Under their production and media company Sangament Media, they have curated music and co-presented events for Celebrate Brooklyn, South Asian Lodge at Sundance, Lincoln Center CityParks SummerStage, Sona Restaurant, Spotify, DJ City, and Audiomack. The have an undergraduate degree in Urban Studies from Queens College and a Master of Science in Comparative Media from MIT. They live in Jackson Heights and are happy to share their favorite food recommendations.

“At some point, I turned the corner and said, well, I’m older now and it’s my responsibility to help and mentor people in any way I can, and be open and generous. Because I didn’t have that doesn’t mean I can’t give it back. That’s part of community.”

DJ Rekha
