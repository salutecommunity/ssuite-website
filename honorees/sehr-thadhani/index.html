Sehr Thadhani

Chief Growth Officer, Nasdaq

S.SUITE Operator

S.SUITE Honors by SALUTE

An executive whose judgment and execution transform how an organization works, grows, and delivers.

Why we honor Sehr Thadhani

For reimagining how an institution connects with the world—and making the intersection of growth, culture, and human experience a source of transformation.

Biography

Sehr Thadhani is an award-winning business builder and growth executive whose career spans technology, capital markets, consumer and media, with leadership roles at some of the world's most influential companies, including Nasdaq, Google and NBCUniversal, and advisory work for global brands such as Ford, Estée Lauder, Samsung and EY.

As Nasdaq's first Chief Growth Officer, she architected and led a cross-functional growth organization with P&L oversight spanning commercial strategy, IPO and listings growth, go-to-market, strategic partnerships, market development and brand. She also advised more than 100 high-growth companies on scaling and IPO readiness, while elevating Nasdaq's cultural relevance and ensuring it remained the exchange of choice for the next generation of market-defining companies. Previously, she led new product development at NBCUniversal, strategic initiatives at Google, and advised global companies on emerging technologies, new business models and market opportunities.

Sehr holds an MBA from Columbia Business School and a BA in Business and Music from Indiana University. She is an Aspen Institute Finance Leaders Fellow, serves on the board of the Metropolitan Opera and advises Lincoln Center's Technology Council. Sehr is a "third culture kid" who grew up across multiple countries and a classically trained vocalist who has performed with the Washington Opera, at Carnegie Hall and on Broadway.

“Think about how you as a leader are poised to address big questions facing your business. What’s your innovation roadmap and where are you experimenting? Are these cultural, technological and business challenges that need to be solved?”

Sehr Thadhani
