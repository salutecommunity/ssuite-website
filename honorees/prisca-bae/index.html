Prisca Bae

Chief Partnerships Officer, The Asian American Foundation

S.SUITE Ally

S.SUITE Honors by SALUTE

Recognizing leaders for sustained allyship that advances South Asian leaders into meaningful roles and opportunities.

Why we honor Prisca Bae

For turning partnerships into a platform for belonging—and bringing institutions, philanthropy, and community together around greater safety, visibility, and opportunity.

Biography

Prisca Bae is a noted social-impact leader who excels at forging collaborations between corporations, government, nonprofits, and philanthropic initiatives. She is a founding team member of The Asian American Foundation (TAAF) and currently serves as the organization's Chief Partnerships Officer. Prior to TAAF, Prisca held leadership roles at PepsiCo, Seneca Women, Newsweek and Goldman Sachs. Prisca began her career at Latham & Watkins and served as Federal Law Clerk to Senior District Court Judge William Hoeveler in Florida's Southern District. During her time at PepsiCo, under CEO Indra Nooyi's landmark Performance with Purpose strategy, she spearheaded the development and launch of a $100 million investment in women and girls globally, while also helping to advance gender parity and pay equity initiatives across the organization. While at Goldman Sachs, she launched Goldman Sachs Gives, a billion-dollar donor advised fund available to current and former partners of the firm.

She currently serves on the board of the Columbia Alumni Association. Prisca is a graduate of Columbia University and the UC Berkeley School of Law. She was born in South Korea, raised in Chicago, and currently resides in New York City.

“People have to learn more about your community, who you are, who your parents are, and who your grandparents are, for them to understand that you're just like them.”

Prisca Bae
