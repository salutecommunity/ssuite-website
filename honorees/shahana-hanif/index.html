Council Member Shahana Hanif

District 39, New York City Council

S.SUITE Rising

S.SUITE Honors by SALUTE

An emerging leader already operating with meaningful scope, creating change and lifting others as she advances.

Why we honor Council Member Shahana Hanif

For bringing lived experience into public leadership—and working to make civic institutions more responsive to the communities whose lives they shape.

Biography

Council Member Shahana Hanif made history in 2021 as the first Bangladeshi and Muslim woman elected to the New York City Council, representing District 39 in Brooklyn. In her first term, she served as Co-Chair of the Council’s Progressive Caucus, leading successful budget fights to invest in housing, mental health services, non-carceral justice, and immigrant communities. Advancing disability justice has been a central priority throughout her work in office.

Council Member Hanif currently chairs the Council’s Committee on Disabilities and Accessibility. Her journey into public service began after she was diagnosed with Lupus at seventeen, an illness that eventually required both hip replacements and a left shoulder replacement. After being denied Access-A-Ride, she experienced firsthand how difficult it can be to navigate New York City with an inaccessible subway system and prohibitively expensive car service options. That experience continues to shape her work advancing disability justice and accessibility across the city.

During her time in office, she has passed landmark laws expanding abortion access, advancing climate justice through universal composting, strengthening worker protections, and investing in safety upgrades for survivors of domestic violence. She formerly served as Chair of the Immigration Committee and Co-chair of the Task Force to Combat Hate. In 2025, she was re-elected to serve her final term as Council Member.

“I entered the political arena without seeing many elected officials with disabilities, and without seeing many elected officials who looked like me. I wondered how much I would have to advocate for myself while also being an effective representative. But I didn’t let those uncertainties hold me back.”

Council Member Shahana Hanif
