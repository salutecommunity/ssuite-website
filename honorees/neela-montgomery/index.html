Neela Montgomery

Board Director, Ahold Delhaize · Logitech · Kibo AI

S.SUITE Director

S.SUITE Honors by SALUTE

A board leader shaping strategy, oversight, and accountability—and opening the boardroom to more South Asian leaders.

Why we honor Neela Montgomery

For bringing the perspective of a global operator into the boardroom, connecting transformation on the ground with the decisions that shape an organization’s future.

Biography

Neela Montgomery is a global public and private markets CEO and board director with leadership experience spanning consumer, healthcare, technology, and retail industries.

Today Neela serves as a Senior Advisor at Alix Partners across Retail, Consumer & Healthcare and serves on various Boards including Logitech, Ahold Delhaize and Kibo Commerce. She was recently the CEO of Orveon Global, a beauty collective including Bare Minerals and Laura Mercier, where she led a rapid transformation back to profitability and rebuilt the executive leadership team. She was previously a Venture Partner at Greycroft working with high-growth startups in artificial intelligence, consumer technology and healthcare.

Earlier, Neela played a pivotal role during the COVID-19 pandemic as President of CVS Pharmacy, overseeing 10,000 pharmacies and 190,000 colleagues in a $90 billion division. Under her leadership, CVS administered more than 55 million vaccines and tests, delivering critical healthcare access to millions of Americans while achieving record revenue and profit growth.

As CEO of Crate & Barrel Holdings, Neela led a multi-year turnaround that returned the company to profitability, grew revenues from ~$1.5B to ~$2.4B, and doubled sales at CB2. She repositioned the heritage brand with a digital-first strategy, building an omnichannel model where e-commerce reached more than 60% of sales, launching new concepts, and expanding internationally. Her leadership strengthened sourcing, modernized culture, and developed a next-generation leadership team, transforming Crate & Barrel into a growth platform.

Previously, Neela served as an Executive Board Member at Otto Group, overseeing multiple consumer brands and acting as Executive Chair of Crate & Barrel. She began her career at Tesco PLC, where over her 12 year tenure she held senior roles across strategy, merchandising, e-commerce, and international markets.

Neela is an experienced board director, bringing expertise in CEO leadership, strategy, digital transformation, customer engagement, and governance. She currently serves on the boards of Logitech (Compensation Committee, formerly Audit), Ahold Delhaize (Technology & Audit Committees), Kibo (Vista Equity company), St. Jude’s Research Hospital (Audit, Compensation, Investment Committees) and Boston Medical Center – WellSense (Trustee). She previously served on Squarespace public board (Audit, Compensation, and Special Committee for $7.8B take-private), Fetch and National Retail Federation Boards.

Neela has an MBA from INSEAD and a BA in English Lit from Oxford University. She is a Henry Crown Fellow at the Aspen Institute, reflecting her commitment to leadership and social impact.

“We need diverse voices and perspectives in the boardroom, where important decisions are made. As more South Asian women enter that space, I am proud to champion the next generation of leaders who will reach heights we cannot yet imagine.”

Neela Montgomery
